Why we exist

London has thousands of pizzas. Almost none for our table.

Ask for a pizza with no egg, no dairy, no onion, no garlic and no root vegetables, and watch the menu fall apart. Each kind of place gets one part right and misses the rest.

1

Vegan pizzerias

No meat, no dairy. But onion and garlic often run through the sauces, the oils and the toppings. Vegan isn't the same as Jain or Satvik.

2

The big chains

Great at speed. But a Jain order turns into a list of things to take off, and you still can't be sure what's in the base or the sauce.

3

Indian vegetarian kitchens

They understand Jain and Satvik food perfectly. But they're not pizzerias, and pizza night shouldn't be a compromise.

So families picked toppings off, or didn't order at all. We think that's wrong. Pizza is meant to be shared.

Our story

Two years in a Harrow kitchen.

Green Leaf Pizza comes from Healthy Bakes, the eggless, free-from bakery Rituja started at home in Harrow.

She spent two years developing natural, free-from recipes, because every alternative she tried asked you to accept less flavour. She refused to. Healthy food doesn't have to taste bad; it just has to be made properly.

Pizza was the obvious next question. Could the same care make a pizza with a crisp, blistered edge and a rich tomato heart, with no egg, no dairy, no onion, no garlic, and no compromise? It could.

Nothing taken away from the taste. Nobody left out of the table.
